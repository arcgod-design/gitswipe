## The user push that changed the plan

"Do we seriously need Tailscale?" - the user rejected third-party VPN friction for the product vision (setup laptop + install app + key + model = walla done) and questioned the pricing. Brutal verification of all 8 candidates (docs/TRANSPORT-DECISION.md):

- Factually corrected: the 1000-minute limit is ephemeral-CI-only; laptop+phone are unlimited on Tailscale free. Recorded so pricing never gets re-litigated.
- Dropped Tailscale anyway - the UX vision + demo-day operational risk + third-party dependency objections are valid on their own.
- Physics: no zero-infrastructure option exists (NAT on both ends). Three categories: VPN mesh (apps on both devices - dead), public tunnel (laptop-only - works now), owned relay (the walla endgame - orca pattern, contract s24).
- Selected: demo = Cloudflare Quick Tunnel primary + phone-hotspot fallback; product = `jarvisd share` (cloudflared subprocess + QR in terminal) then our own thin relay in v1.x. Locked as ADR 0009.
