3. Post-approval recheck re-ran the full review and refused its own APPROVAL_REQUIRED as "policy drift" — recheck now denies only on drift-to-DENY via the shared denyCheck core.
4. The twice-learned lesson applied: @jarvis/security deps fully declared up front (the audit's hoisting hazard).
